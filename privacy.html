Privacy Policy — TestSpace

Bot: @testspace_bot

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

  • Telegram user id (used only on the server; not shown to other students)
  • Display name (from Telegram or set with /setname)
  • Exam answers, scores, time taken, and attempt timestamps

How we use data

  • To run exams, calculate scores, and show results
  • To build class leaderboards (first attempt only)
  • Teachers manage exams; students only access exams via teacher-shared links

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Scores and display names may appear on class leaderboards after an exam ends.

Retention & removal

Data is stored while the service is active. Contact the teacher or bot owner to request removal of your exam records.
